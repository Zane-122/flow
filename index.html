How to draw

Arrows — just drag on empty space. Let go and it snaps into a clean curve, or a straight line if you meant straight.
Shapes — hold W and drag. Tap W to cycle shape, Q to cycle preset sizes (Free/S/M/L — even while dragging).
Select — press 1 (or V) to select, move, and delete (⌫) things. Tools also switch with keys 1–6.
Text — press T (or double-click) and click to drop text. Double-click a shape to label it.
Fill — pick a color, hit F, click a shape.
Connect — arrows near a shape snap to it. Select an arrow and drag its round end handles to detach or re-attach.
Move / Pan — drag anything to move it. Use the Pan tool (H) or Space+drag to move around the canvas; scroll to pan, ⌘+scroll to zoom. ⌫ deletes selected.
